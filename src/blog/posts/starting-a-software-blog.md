This is the start of a place for notes on the software I build, the tradeoffs behind it, and the things I learn along the way.

I expect most posts to sit somewhere between a field note and a small technical write-up: practical enough to be useful later, but informal enough that publishing does not become its own project.

Topics I expect to come back to:

- building focused iOS and macOS apps
- shaping indie products from small ideas
- using Python where it makes the backend simpler
- working with AI tools without letting them erase taste or judgement
- the tiny implementation details that make software feel better

For now, the important part is having a lightweight place to write. The rest can evolve as the habit does.
